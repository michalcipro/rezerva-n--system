# Czech and Slovak market for online booking systems: physiotherapy, massage and fitness/conditioning studios (2025–2026)

*Method note for the report writer: all data was collected on 2026-10-14. WebFetch failed with DNS errors on every domain, and direct HTTP was blocked by the egress proxy (403). As a result, every fact below comes from search-engine result pages and their snippets/summaries, many of them restricted to the vendor's own domain, and not from full page reads. Where the search summary did not tie a claim to one specific URL, the bullet says so and cites the group of pages it came from. The search budget ran out before SuperSaaS, SimplyBook.me, Reservatic features, Clubspire vendor pricing, Bookio pass details, slot granularity and e-mail marketing could be covered (see the Gaps sections). Prices are given as found, and the bullet states whether they include or exclude VAT ("bez DPH" means excluding VAT; Czech VAT is 21 %).*

## 1. Which booking systems are most used in CZ/SK by physiotherapists, massage therapists, fitness/yoga/pilates studios and course providers, and are they active in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Self-pay Czech physiotherapists and masseurs mostly book through general appointment SaaS, chiefly **Reservio**, **Reservanto** and **Reenio**, with **MyFox**, **Bookio** (SK), **Noona** and newer entrants such as Salona also present. Gyms and group-class studios lean on **Clubspire** (credit plus permanentky). Course and kroužek providers, such as swim schools and language schools, use dedicated omluvenky/náhrady systems (**Auksys**, **Webooker**). Clinics that bill health insurers use physiotherapy-specific record systems (**Fyzion**, **FYZIOPAD**) or classic ambulantní software from CGM (MEDICUS/MEDISTAR), sometimes with a patient-communication layer (**Medevio**). Every system named here showed signs of activity in 2025–2026.

### Inferences
- The subdomain counts suggest that for **self-pay physiotherapy and massage in CZ, Reservio and Reservanto are the most visible general tools**, with Reenio third. Brno physiotherapists in particular cluster on Reservio. This rests on search-index sampling, not market-share data.
- Reservio's very high review counts (for example 2,842 reviews on one clinic) suggest that its marketplace and review layer is a real acquisition and trust channel for physiotherapists. A custom platform loses that unless it replaces it with Google reviews or similar.
- There is a clear **three-way split**: (a) appointment SaaS for 1:1 sessions, (b) gym and course systems for credit, permanentky and omluvenky, and (c) medical record and insurer-billing systems. A clinic offering physiotherapy, 1:1 and small-group conditioning, and massage today has to stretch one tool, such as Reservanto Professional or Reenio, or combine several.

### Gaps
- No market-share statistics for CZ/SK booking tools were found. Prevalence is inferred from vendor-hosted subdomains surfaced by search.
- **SuperSaaS, SimplyBook.me and Booksy CZ** were not researched because the search budget ran out. Their 2025–2026 CZ activity, CZK pricing and features remain unverified.
- **Reservatic** (SK/CZ) is confirmed to exist with 1/3/12-month licences (see Q2), but its CZ/SK physiotherapy usage was not checked.
- Pilates and yoga-specific CZ usage was only incidentally covered (Eka Yoga Space and DVA jóga on Reservanto).

## 2. Per-system feature map: credits, permanentky, předplatné and vouchers; omluvenky and náhrady; storno; waitlist; payments; invoicing; statistics; marketing; pricing in CZK/EUR; strengths and complaints

### Takeaway
Among the general Czech tools, **Reservanto (Professional, 499 Kč/month bez DPH)** is the only one found that natively bundles **permanentky, kredity, vouchery and náhrady** (makeup credit from cancelled paid bookings), plus a **náhradníci** waitlist. **Reenio** offers customer credit, permanentky (implemented as a special voucher), alternates and GoPay/Comgate/Stripe, but meters SMS and payments through its own "reenio credits". **Reservio** is strongest in marketplace, apps and simplicity, but its user and API features are gated to Pro, and no confirmed waitlist or omluvenky model was found. **MyFox** focuses on deposits and no-show protection rather than passes. Physiotherapy-specific tools (Fyzion, FYZIOPAD) add insurer billing and records but show no evidence of permanentky or group-class logic.

#### Reservanto (CZ)
- **Tier contents.** Lite adds e-mail reminders, recurring events, basic statistics and the Poska cash register. **Professional** is the first tier with **"Permanentky, Kredity, Vouchery, Náhrady"**, extended customer management and Google Calendar sync. **Premium** adds multi-user access, permissions, a loyalty programme, import/export and API. — [reservanto.cz/cenik](https://reservanto.cz/cenik)
- **SMS and e-mail fees** are per piece and vary by tier, for example 1.50 Kč on Basic and 1.10 Kč on Premium. — [reservanto.cz/cenik](https://reservanto.cz/cenik)
- **Payments.** Customers can pay with permanentky and kredit. — [reservanto.cz](https://reservanto.cz/)
- **Transaction fees** on online payments run 3–6 % by tier: 6 % on Basic, 3 % on Professional and Premium. — [zaptime.cz](https://zaptime.cz/blog/nejlepsi-rezervacni-system/), [alteg.io](https://alteg.io/en/blog/best-booking-system-czechia/)
- **Gateways.** Card, bank transfer and cryptocurrency payments are advertised. A merchant's own **Pays** account can be connected. ThePay, Comgate and GoPay were not confirmed by the search. — [reservanto.cz/vlastnosti](https://reservanto.cz/vlastnosti), [Pays blog](https://reservanto.cz/o-nas/blog/pays-nova-platebni-metoda-n462478.htm)
- **Náhrady (makeups).** Enabled in Settings → General. For each service the owner chooses which services a náhrada may be used for. For courses the owner can follow the service setting or configure each course separately, **including a time limit**. When a **paid** booking is cancelled by the customer or by staff, it becomes a "Náhrada". Customers see náhrady in a "Náhrady" tab in their booking management, and only paid bookings can become náhrady. Náhrady also act as a payment method. The help page is ~670 days old. — [Reservanto nápověda: Náhrady](https://reservanto.cz/napoveda/platebni-metody/nahrady-n460566.htm)
- **Náhradníci (waitlist).** Enabled in Settings → General. It is aimed mainly at sports venues but also at language courses, lectures and capacity-limited events, and is available in the "Skupina zákazníků v pevný čas" mode. — [Reservanto nápověda: Náhradníci](https://reservanto.cz/napoveda/pro-zakazniky/nahradnici-n460601.htm)
- **Vouchers** can be sold as a discount, a free service, a permanentka or a credit top-up. Sales through the voucher shop carry Reservanto's online-payment fee, and connecting your own gateway to the shop costs a monthly fee. The blog post is ~5 years old. — [blog.reservanto.cz/vouchery](https://blog.reservanto.cz/vouchery)
- **Invoicing and accounting.** Customers can automatically receive an accounting document when paying for bookings or permanentky. **Fakturoid and iDoklad connect only via Make** (an automation tool), not natively. Poska is Reservanto's cash register. — [reservanto.cz/cenik](https://reservanto.cz/cenik), [Make blog](https://reservanto.cz/o-nas/blog/propojeni-reservanta-s-make-n462602.htm), [reservanto.cz/poska](https://reservanto.cz/poska)

## 4. Which Czech systems handle omluvenky a náhrady (common in courses, kroužky, swimming and exercise classes) best, and how exactly do they model it?

### Takeaway
Course-oriented **Auksys** is the purest "náhradový systém": a timely excuse creates a credit that can be spent on a makeup in a free slot, and a watch on a full class reserves a credit and returns it if nobody drops out. **Reservanto** models náhrady inside a general booking SaaS: a cancelled **paid** booking turns into a "Náhrada" that works like a payment method, with per-service eligibility and per-course time limits, plus a "Náhradníci" waitlist. **Webooker** ties excuses to attendance, with makeups limited to the current semester. **CLUBSPIRE** and **Reenio** model waitlists and credit, but no explicit omluvenka-to-náhrada entitlement was found for them.

### Cited Findings
- **Auksys mechanics.**
  - Lessons are excused only through the system; phone excuses are not accepted. — [kajmanek.auksys.com](https://kajmanek.auksys.com/master/index.php)
  - Excuse deadlines are set per club, for example 6:00 on the lesson day or 21:00 the previous day. Unused makeups expire, and late or unexcused absences give no makeup right. — [zabickytrebic.cz/auksys](https://www.zabickytrebic.cz/auksys/), [dcvlnka.cz](https://dcvlnka.cz/omluvy-a-nahrady/)
  - **An excuse adds a credit that can be used for a makeup.** If you set a watch on a full ("hlídaná") lesson and nobody excuses, the credit is returned. — [step.zonavpohybu.cz FAQ](https://step.zonavpohybu.cz/caste-dotazy)
  - For credit-package courses the customer buys a number of credits and picks free slots. — [webprosokoly.cz](https://webprosokoly.cz/)
  - Credits can only be used after the whole course fee is paid. — [dcvlnka.cz](https://dcvlnka.cz/omluvy-a-nahrady/)
  - Rodinné centrum Vlnka calls it a "náhradový systém Auksys". — [rcvlnka-plzen.cz](https://www.rcvlnka-plzen.cz/nahradovy-system-auksys/)
- **Swim-school rule range** (the search summary's synthesis across operator pages; not mapped to individual pages):
  - Excuse deadline typically about 20:00 the day before, or 7:00/8:00 on the lesson day depending on start time; a late excuse forfeits the lesson.
  - Makeups by free capacity: within the running course plus the first 4 weeks of the next one, or within 60 days after the course ends.
  - No refunds or transfers after the course starts; limited credit validity.
  - Online makeup booking only after paying the full course; some operators allow unlimited makeups for properly excused lessons.

### Inferences
- The **best-modelled náhrady** are in Auksys (credit per excuse plus a watch/hold with automatic credit return) and Reservanto (makeup as a payment method with eligibility mapping and time limits). For a physiotherapy and conditioning clinic, a combined model would fit: excuse before deadline X → makeup credit valid for N days → usable only for eligible services or groups → watch on a full class reserves the credit and auto-books or returns it.
- The parameters operators actually use are: excuse deadline (relative hours before start, or an absolute time the previous day), makeup validity (current course or semester, +4 weeks, 60 days after the end), payment precondition (course fully paid), eligibility (which classes count), cap (unlimited or limited), and refund policy (none, or after N consecutive absences with a doctor's note).

## 5. What gaps or recurring complaints exist in Czech tools that a custom platform could address?

### Takeaway
Recurring friction points:
- Key features sit behind higher tiers (multi-user, API, Google sync, loyalty, templates).
- Payment commissions are high (Reservanto 3–6 %; marketplace commissions of 20–45 % at Fresha and Booksy).
- Invoicing integration is weak (Make-based or none).
- Usage is metered (SMS paid per piece; Reenio blocks the service when credits run out).
- Branding is limited and some UIs look dated (Reservanto).
- Free-plan caps hide data (Reservio).
- No tool cleanly combines physiotherapy 1:1 appointments, group classes with permanentky or credit, omluvenky/náhrady and insurer or medical records.

### Inferences
Opportunities for the custom platform, derived from the findings above:
1. **One client account with a unified wallet.** Monetary credit, session-based permanentky (per service type, for example physio, 1:1 training, group class, massage) and makeup credits held separately, all visible to the owner, with validity and expiry rules.
2. **First-class omluvenky and náhrady** using the operator parameters found (deadlines, validity windows, eligibility, payment preconditions, caps) plus a waitlist that auto-promotes. CZ tools either lack this or bury it in higher tiers.
3. **Configurable storno policy** per service and per client group: free cancellation window, fixed or percentage fee, forfeit of a permanentka entry, deposits only for risky clients (the MyFox and Salona approach).
4. **Native Czech payment and accounting.** A direct GoPay, Comgate, ThePay or Stripe contract avoids the 3–6 % platform commission; direct Fakturoid or iDoklad API invoicing replaces Make-based workarounds.
5. **No per-seat or per-feature gating** for a single clinic, and no marketplace commission.
6. **Variable durations with buffers and 15-minute slot granularity** across therapists and rooms; Reenio-style resource collision checks are a baseline.
7. **Owner statistics** on permanentky liability (unused prepaid sessions), náhrady outstanding, no-show and late-cancel rates, and therapist utilisation. None of the sources showed these reports as a selling point.
8. If insurer care is ever added: export or integration toward Fyzion or FYZIOPAD or MEDICUS/MEDISTAR, or a Medevio-like layer, rather than rebuilding zdravotnická dokumentace.

### Gaps
- Independent review data (Google, Capterra, G2, Facebook groups) on Czech booking tools was not collected, because fetch access failed and the search budget ran out. The complaints above come mostly from competitor-written comparisons and one affiliate review.
- No direct evidence was found on design and UX quality beyond Alteg's "outdated design" remark on Reservanto and the existence of mobile apps (Reservio web/Android/iOS, CLUBSPIRE, Webooker, Noona kiosk).
- GDPR and health-data handling practices of the general booking tools (they store anamnéza via custom forms, for example Reenio) were not assessed.
