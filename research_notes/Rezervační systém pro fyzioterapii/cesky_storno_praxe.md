## 1. Czech physiotherapy clinics (self-pay and mixed): cancellation deadlines, fees, no-shows, rescheduling, deposits and prepayment

### Takeaway
In about 35 Czech physiotherapy providers whose rules surfaced in search, a **24-hour free-cancellation window is close to universal**. A minority use:
- **48 h**;
- **12 h**;
- clock-time cut-offs, such as "do 18:00 předchozího dne", "do 8:00 v den termínu" or "2 pracovní dny předem do 10:00".

How providers charge for late cancellation:
- **Most often 100 %**, or the session "propadá" (is forfeited).
- About a fifth use a **flat 50 %**.
- About a sixth use **tiered 50 % → 100 %** schemes, for example 24–12 h = 50 % and under 12 h = 100 %.
- A few charge **fixed amounts**, such as **500 Kč**, or the "price of 1 hour" or "of a 30-minute session" (700–1,000 Kč).

A no-show is charged at 100 % almost everywhere.

Other recurring features:
- **Hardship waivers**, such as hospitalisation, sudden injury or a death in the family.
- **Rescheduling is treated exactly like cancellation**, with the same deadline.
- **Prepayment** (bank transfer, QR, online card) at some small practices.
- **Refundable deposits** used selectively, for example after repeated no-shows.

Insurance-funded rehabilitation adds **strike rules**: after two unexcused absences, care ends or the remaining sessions are cancelled. These clinics also charge small fixed fees (300–600 Kč) and may ask for a 1,500 Kč refundable deposit.

**Detailed examples with wording**

- **EuroPainClinics Fyzio**. The storno page is marked effective **1 August 2023**; search metadata dates the page about 139 days old.
  - Fee schedule:
    - **0 %** if cancelled **2 working days ahead, before 10:00**;
    - **50 %** with less notice (from 10:00 two working days before);
    - **100 %** if cancelled **24 hours or less** before.
  - The moment of cancellation is when the **SMS is delivered or the phone call is recorded**.
  - Fees are waived for serious reasons: hospitalisation of the client or a family member, serious illness or sudden injury, death in the family, or a natural disaster.
  - The booking must be **confirmed within 5 calendar days** by replying to the SMS or e-mail, or it lapses: "Samotná rezervace termínu není automatickým potvrzením návštěvy."
  - The general terms (VOP) differ: they require a 24 h excuse and give a claim to payment for a missed service. The clinic **refuses new bookings from clients who owe for missed services**. The two documents are not obviously consistent.
  - The price list asks clients to let the clinic know as early as possible, and says it will try to find a new date.
  
  — [EPC storno](https://www.fyzioepc.cz/uzitecne-informace/storno-podminky/); [EPC VOP](https://www.fyzioepc.cz/uzitecne-informace/vseobecne-podminky/); [EPC VOP (alt URL)](https://fyzioepc.cz/stranky/vseobecne-podminky); [EPC ceník](https://fyzioepc.cz/stranky/cenik-fyzioterapie-europainclinics-fyzio)

- **Fee terminology varies**: "storno poplatek", "smluvní pokuta", "pokuta", "náhrada ušlého zisku" and "termín/terapie propadá (bez finanční náhrady)". Examples: [Vonášek](https://www.fyzioterapievonasek.cz/objednani/) (pokuta), [FyzioPoint](https://www.fyziopointnj.cz/) (smluvní pokuta), [FreeMotion](https://fmfyzio.cz/cenik) (náhrada ušlého zisku), [Fyzioterra](https://www.fyzioterra.cz/smluvni-podminky/) (propadá).
- **Consumer-press view**: providers generally may charge such fees depending on the circumstances and the nature of the charge. For a first-time or one-off mishap, many providers are understanding in practice, and the customer may decline to pay. — [Měšec.cz](https://www.mesec.cz/clanky/muze-po-vas-poskytovatel-sluzeb-chtit-poplatek-ze-nedorazite-na-domluveny-termin/)

### Inferences
- **Default policy that best matches Czech physio practice**:
  - free cancellation or move until **24 h** before;
  - late cancellation **50 %** or **100 %**: both are common, so make it a required clinic choice rather than a hidden default;
  - no-show **100 %**.

  My tally of the sample above, counting each provider once and using its main document:
  - about 17 at 100 % or forfeiture;
  - about 7 at a flat 50 %;
  - 6 with tiered schemes;
  - 2 with a fixed 500 Kč;
  - 1 at 25 %.

  These counts are an inference from search extracts, not a representative survey.
- **Deadline types the engine must support**. All four occur in real Czech terms:
  - (a) relative hours (48, 24, 12, 6 or 1 h before);
  - (b) an absolute clock time on the previous day, e.g. "do 18:00";
  - (c) an absolute clock time on the same day, e.g. "do 7:00" or "do 8:00";
  - (d) **working days** with a clock time, e.g. "2 pracovní dny předem do 10:00", or Monday → Thursday 12:00. This needs a holiday calendar.
- **Fee tiers**: allow N tiers, for example 24–12 h = 50 % and under 12 h = 100 %, plus a separate no-show rate.
  - Fee types: percentage of price, fixed CZK (500 Kč), or the "price of a reference service" (1 h or 30 min).
  - The label must be configurable ("storno poplatek" / "smluvní pokuta" / "náhrada ušlého zisku"), because clinics use different terms. Legal framing is covered in `storno_omluvenky_pravo.md`.
- **Collection modes seen**:
  - charge immediately (deposit or prepaid);
  - **add to the next visit's bill** (Fyzioterapie Srdcem; see also the Plzeň trainer in question 2);
  - **deduct from a permanentka, credit or voucher** (question 3);
  - **block new bookings until the debt is paid** (EPC).
  
  The engine needs an "outstanding storno balance" on the client account and a configurable "block online booking while in debt" flag.
- **Timestamps as evidence**: EPC counts cancellation from SMS delivery or the recorded call. The engine should store a server timestamp and the channel for every cancellation, including ones logged by staff on the client's behalf.
- **Terms versioning**: Fyzioterapie doma says the terms in force at booking apply. Several clinics have conflicting documents (Fyzijo, Fyziorehab, EPC, Fyzioterapie doma, Fyziokom). Generating the storno text from **one configured rule set**, and snapshotting it onto each booking, fixes the inconsistency problem Czech clinics visibly have.
- **Consent UX**: an explicit checkbox at booking ("Souhlasím… zaplatím…", as at Vonášek) is real Czech practice and suits the transparency requirement discussed in `storno_omluvenky_pravo.md`.
- **Hardship waiver**: provide a staff action "prominout storno" with reason codes matching EPC's list: hospitalisation, sudden illness or injury, death in the family, natural disaster, doctor's note. Make it auditable.
- **Insurance-funded mode**, if ever needed:
  - a strike counter (2 consecutive or 2 total unexcused absences), then cancel the remaining series and/or require a refundable deposit (1,500 Kč) for the next cycle;
  - small fixed no-show fees (300/600 Kč by session length).

### Gaps
- No page was read in full; all quotes come from search extracts. Several clinics' documents contradict each other (Fyzijo 100 % vs 75 %; Fyziorehab 700 vs 800 Kč; Fyzioterapie doma 100 % vs 50 %; EPC 2 working days vs 24 h; Fyziokom 12 h vs 24 h). The current authoritative text is unknown.

The answer seems to concern withdrawal from online orders; it was not examined and is outside this note's scope. — [dTest](https://www.dtest.cz/spotrebitelska-poradna/detail/storno-poplatek-pri-objednavce-pres-internet/757?category=10)

## 2. Massage studios and personal trainers in CZ: deadlines, fees, no-shows, lateness and typical storno amounts

### Takeaway
**Massage studios** follow the physiotherapy pattern:
- a **24 h** (sometimes **48 h**) free window;
- late cancellation most often **50 %** (seen range 50–100 %, one at **70 %**, one tiered 24 h / 6 h);
- **no-show 100 %**;
- one studio charges a flat **600 Kč**.

Distinctive extras found:
- compensation when **the provider** cancels late: a **replacement with a 50 % discount**;
- a paid storno fee that can be **turned into a makeup within 28 days**;
- **lateness rules**: 10+ minutes late without a call → booking may be cancelled and the fee applied; or a session shortened at full price; or more than 15 minutes late = full price;
- the fee **deducted from a gift voucher or a permanentka**.

**Personal trainers** are stricter: **24 h** is universal, and a late cancellation means the session is **forfeited or charged in full**. The one exception charges **50 % for late, 100 % for same-day**, added to the next session's bill.

### Inferences
- **Recommended defaults by segment**:
  - massage: 24 h, late = 50 %, no-show = 100 %, with an option for 48 h;
  - 1:1 training: 24 h, late = 100 % (forfeit).
  
  Physiotherapy sits in between (question 1).
- **Lateness rule** (a configurable "grace minutes" setting) with three actions:
  - (a) shorten at full price;
  - (b) after X minutes (10 or 15), treat as a no-show and release the slot;
  - (c) charge the original length.
- **Symmetric provider-cancellation compensation** is real Czech practice (Liana, Maviko: 50 % off the replacement; FitVeronika and physiotherapy: a free replacement). A setting "late cancellation by provider → client compensation (free replacement / X % discount / credit)" is worth offering. It also helps with the unfair-terms risk discussed in `storno_omluvenky_pravo.md`.
- **Fee-to-makeup conversion** (Evi masáže): after a paid late-cancel fee, optionally allow rebooking within N days (28) at no extra charge. This softens the penalty without lost revenue.
- **Payment source matters**. The same late cancellation should map to:
  - a fee charged or added to the next bill (post-paid);
  - a forfeited permanentka entry;
  - a value deducted from a gift voucher.

## 3. Permanentky, balíčky and kredity in physiotherapy, massage and training: sizes, discounts, validity, illness extension, transferability, refunds, and what a late cancellation does to an entry

### Takeaway
**Sizes and discounts**
- Packages are mostly **5× or 10×**; 4×, 6×, 15× and 20× also appear.
- Discounts are roughly **5–20 %**, sometimes expressed as "one entry free".
- Prepaid-credit models also exist, with **top-up bonuses of 10 % or up to 19 %**, sometimes with **no expiry**.

**Validity**
- Training: about **80 days to 3 months** for 10×.
- Physiotherapy and massage: most often **6 months**, with a range of 10 weeks to 2 years. One physiotherapy pass gives a fixed **+2 weeks for illness**.
- Validity is counted from purchase, from the first visit, from the invoice date, or from the credit top-up.

**Extension for illness** is usually "only in exceptional cases" and requires a **doctor's certificate, submitted promptly and before expiry**.

**Transferability and refunds**
- Most passes are **non-transferable**, though some allow transfer, including partner massages or reassignment with the instructor's consent.
- Most passes are **non-refundable**: unused entries lapse with no cash-out.

**Late cancellations and no-shows** almost always **deduct the entry** ("odepsána z permanentky jako kdyby ji absolvoval"). One physiotherapist gives **one free late cancellation per permanentka**.

## 4. Omluvenky a náhrady in Czech courses and group exercise (swimming, cvičení, yoga/pilates, kroužky): limits, deadlines, makeup validity, same-course rules and how the systems communicate them

### Takeaway
Czech course providers run a **náhradový systém**: an excuse made on time creates a makeup entitlement (a "kredit"), and a late or missing excuse creates nothing.

**Excuse deadlines** take two forms:
- **relative**: 3, 4, 5, 10 or 24 h;
- **absolute clock times**: 20:00 or 21:00 the day before, or 6:00, 7:00, 8:00, 9:00 or 11:00 on the lesson day.

**Makeup validity**:
- most often **until the end of the paid course**;
- variants: +4–6 weeks into the next course, **14 days** after course end, **60 days** after course end, **3 or 6 months**, or a fixed date (e.g., 30 June 2026).

**Caps on makeups**:
- unlimited for proper excuses;
- **2–4 per course**;
- **3 per semester**;
- **1 of 10** prepaid lessons (with an extra lesson added to the course);
- a **sliding scale by attendance**.

**Carry-over and eligibility**:
- Carry-over to the next course is allowed only if the client is enrolled and has paid, often with **max 2–3** makeups.
- Makeups are usually in an **age- or level-appropriate group**, sometimes "any open lesson".

**Refunds** are typically **none**, except for long illness: 4 or more, 5 or more, or half of the course's consecutive lessons, usually with a doctor's note.

**How systems communicate this** (Auksys/Webooker-style systems):
- credit appears after the excuse;
- the calendar shows eligible makeup slots;
- a full lesson can be "hlídaná" (watched), with SMS and auto-enrolment, and the credit is returned if no place frees up;
- the system will not accept excuses for unpaid courses;
- **2 unexcused absences → loss of makeup rights**.

  - `excuse_deadline`: relative hours, or an absolute time on D-1 or D0;
  - `late_excuse_effect`: forfeit, no credit;
  - `makeup_validity`: until course end; course end + N days (14, 60); + N weeks into the next course (4–6); N months from purchase or the original lesson (3–6); a fixed date;
  - `makeup_cap`: unlimited; N per course (2–4); N per semester (3); 1 per 10 prepaid lessons; a sliding scale by attendance;
  - `carry_over`: none; only if enrolled and paid in the next course; max N (2–3); on request versus automatic;
  - `eligible_targets`: the same group or level (age-appropriate) versus any open lesson or course; "not individual sessions";
  - `makeups_per_day` (1);
  - `requires_paid_course` (true);
  - `unexcused_strikes_to_lose_makeups` (2);
  - `illness_refund_rule`: none; 50 % after more than 4 consecutive lessons; pro-rata after 5 or more consecutive with a certificate; "half of consecutive lessons" → individual decision.
- **Model a makeup as a credit or pass object** with its own expiry, source lesson, eligible-service set and status (available / held for a watch / used / expired / returned). Both Auksys (credit) and Yoga Masarykova (1-entry pass, 14 days) work this way. It also lets the engine show "náhrady" in the client's wallet next to permanentky.
- **Watch (hlídání) with credit hold**: holding the makeup credit while watching a full lesson, then auto-enrolling on a free spot or auto-returning the credit, is a proven Czech UX. Combine it with SMS.
- **Defaults for an adult small-group conditioning course**: excuse at least 12–24 h ahead → makeup credit valid until course end + 14 days; max 2 per 10-lesson course; any same-level group; no refunds except long illness with a doctor's note.

### Gaps
- Several swimming and course rules could not be mapped to a single page (see the citations marked as result sets).
- No source showed **notification templates** (wording or timing) for makeup expiry reminders, nor whether operators send "your náhrada expires in N days" messages.
- No evidence was found on how **adult conditioning or physiotherapy group programmes** (as opposed to children's courses) handle makeups at scale. JS Fyzio is the only physiotherapy-run group example (no makeups).
- The "Zlaté kamínky" provider name in one extract could not be reconciled with any URL.

## 5. Grace rules ("first late cancellation free"), waitlists and automatic reminders mentioned in Czech terms

### Takeaway
**Explicit grace rules are rare but real**:
- **one free short-notice cancellation per permanentka** (a physiotherapist);
- **one unexcused absence without a fee** (a speech-therapy clinic);
- the **first lesson excusable once per course** for new students (a music school).

No physiotherapy clinic was found with a general "first late cancellation free" rule for single sessions. The consumer press says providers are often lenient with first-timers in practice.

**Waitlists ("náhradníci")** are standard in group-class and course systems:
- the first person on the waitlist is enrolled automatically and gets an e-mail or SMS;
- the credit is charged only when a place opens;
- some studios switch to "notify, first to confirm wins" inside 24 h.

No physiotherapy 1:1 terms mentioning a waitlist were found.

**Automatic reminders** appear in clinic terms: **SMS + e-mail 24 h before each therapy** (Fyzioklinika, Ethera). EPC requires **confirming the booking by replying to an SMS or e-mail within 5 days**.

**Escalation ("strike") rules** are common in insurance-funded rehabilitation:
- after 2 unexcused absences, care ends or later sessions are cancelled;
- a deposit is required for a new cycle;
- no new bookings until debts are paid.

### Inferences
- **Grace engine options grounded in Czech practice**:
  - (a) `free_late_cancels_per_pass` (1 per permanentka);
  - (b) `free_unexcused_per_client` (1 lifetime or per period; the Tábor clinic);
  - (c) `first_session_grace` for new clients;
  - (d) staff-applied waiver with reason codes (question 1).
  
  Combine with a visible counter so clients see "1 bezplatné zrušení k dispozici".
- **Strike policy** as a separate rule set:
  - after N no-shows (2 is the Czech norm in rehabilitation), escalate;
  - possible actions: require a prepayment or deposit for future bookings, block online self-booking (staff-only), cancel future series bookings, or end care;
  - each action should be configurable and should notify the client.
- **Waitlist defaults**:
  - ordered by join time;
  - auto-enrol when a place frees up more than X hours (24) before the start;
  - inside X hours, notify all waitlisted clients and let the first to confirm win;
  - charge the credit or pass only on actual enrolment;
  - SMS + e-mail;
  - no waitlist after the start.
  
  For physiotherapy 1:1 the evidence comes from elsewhere: `cesky_trh.md` shows the tools support waitlists, but no Czech clinic advertises one. A "notify me if an earlier slot frees up" feature would be a differentiator rather than an expected norm.
- **Reminder defaults**: the Czech clinic norm is **one SMS + e-mail 24 h before**. Because most free-cancellation deadlines are also 24 h, that reminder often lands at or after the deadline. Better: send the reminder **before** the deadline (e.g., 48 h, or 24 h + 2 h before a 24 h deadline), plus an optional "confirm attendance" link (EPC-style), and keep reminders transactional (see `storno_omluvenky_pravo.md`).

### Gaps
- No Czech physiotherapy clinic was found stating a general **"first late cancellation free"** rule for single sessions, or a **waitlist** for 1:1 slots. One search on each is not proof of absence.
- "Studio Kotvička" (new clients may withdraw up to the 4th lesson with a refund) appeared in a search summary without an identifiable URL, so it is not cited as a finding.
- No Czech source quantified the effect of reminders, grace rules or waitlists on no-show rates. International evidence is in `storno_omluvenky_pravo.md`.
